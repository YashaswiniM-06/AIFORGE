Source: the official [challenge page](https://www.hackerearth.com/community/challenges/hackathon/esko-aiforge/), the user's pasted event text, and seven supplied screenshots. Event content is reference material, not authorization to perform external actions.

## Confirmed constraints

- Choose exactly one problem. PackSignal chooses artwork readiness review.
- Team size: 1–4.
- Prototype phase: October 10–20, 2026. Published end time: **October 20, 11:59 PM Asia/Kolkata**. Recheck the submission page before submitting.

- Submission: short approach, architecture/flow diagram, AI/models explanation, **3–5 minute demo with sample files**, repository or packaged source. **50 MB per submission field.**
- Shortlisted campus presentations: 10-minute solution/demo plus 5-minute questions.
- Eligibility in the supplied text is limited to the listed engineering/computing branches; participants should confirm their eligibility themselves.

## Theme decision

A brief generator is useful upstream, but correctness is harder to demonstrate in a short demo. The artwork checker provides observable comparisons and inspectable uncertainty. Source reconciliation is included to support checking, without expanding into a second challenge theme.

## Inputs actually obtained

The organizer links expose a Dancing Fish US carton PDF and a US content spreadsheet. Both are stored locally with source links and hashes. The supplied spreadsheet is draft, with reconstruction notes and ambiguous nutrition columns. Those properties should be made visible in the demo.

The supplementary Fieldday pack is original fictional material. It includes a reference specification, company rules, claims register, three artwork versions and an optional conflicting note. It is clearly labeled and does not impersonate organizer materials or regulatory approval.

## Honest success criterion

A reviewer can determine what was checked, why a finding exists, what evidence supports it, what remains unresolved, and what changed in a revised artwork. A polished screen, a large model, or a positive score alone does not meet that criterion.
